Who we are

Your neighbourhood newsagency

Knightsbridge Newsagency is an independent, family-run newsagency tucked into Knightsbridge Shopping Village in Castle Hill. It's a small shop that packs a lot in: daily papers, a big wall of magazines and puzzle books, greeting cards for every occasion, gifts, toys, stationery and the lottery counter.

Alongside the newsagency staples we're a handy spot for a few everyday errands — a ParcelPoint pick-up and drop-off point, a dry cleaning drop-off, and PO box rental. It's the kind of place where the person behind the counter will come out and help you find what you're after.

We're open seven days a week, with easy parking right at the door. Whether you're grabbing the weekend paper, picking a card on your way past, or having a go on the Lott, you're always welcome to pop in.
